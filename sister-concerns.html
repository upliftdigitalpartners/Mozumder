The Group

Six specialized concerns. One integrated operation.

Each concern leads its domain; together they deliver the end-to-end coverage enterprise shippers require.

01

Mozumder Transport Agency

Established in 2008, Mozumder Transport provides core land transportation across the country. The pool offers various types of vehicles — Prime Mover 10-wheeler, 23-feet covered van, 20-feet container, 20-feet trailer, and two variants of 40-feet trailer (4-axle and 5-axle).

Mozumder Logistics Limited

Our flagship concern — specialized in transportation of project cargoes to the remote corners of the country by road. The fleet includes low-bed trailer, high-bed trailer and heavy cranes for the hardest, heaviest routes. Trusted by the country’s largest industrial operators.
